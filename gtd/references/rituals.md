# Daily Ritual Structure

Four ritual moments anchor the day. Each requires minimum decisions — priorities are
pre-decided, the ritual executes.

---

## The Highlight

One self-directed personal win pre-decided for tomorrow. Not a work task — work priorities
are externally set by the sprint board. The Highlight is the single personal thing you want
to have done by end of day.

Pre-decided at the work signoff (good energy, right moment). Confirmed at morning planning
(not re-decided). Gives personal time a shape without over-scheduling it.

---

## Start working (~8am) — confirm, don't deliberate

1. Work priorities: check Sunsama / sprint board. Externally decided — this is a lookup.
2. Personal Highlight: confirm what was pre-decided at yesterday's work signoff.
3. Open your daily planning view: arrange pre-scheduled personal tasks into available
   calendar gaps. This is arrangement, not decision — tasks were already scheduled at
   work signoff.
4. Sanity check: does the day fit? The planning view shows this visually.

Zero fresh decisions. Both priority streams are pre-decided. Tasks without a scheduled
date won't appear in the planning view — that's a signal the work signoff step was skipped.

/(Current implementation: org-timeblock in Emacs.)/

---

## Post-scrum (~11:45am) — 2–3 min

One question: did the standup change anything? Adjust if yes; back to work if no.

---

## Work signoff (~5pm) — "work brain off"

Two-part ritual:

**Part 1 — sweep (non-negotiable):** Scan email/Slack for anything to capture. Mark what
got done. Explicitly migrate or drop incomplete items. Preview tomorrow's calendar —
can't set a realistic Highlight without knowing what's fixed. Set tomorrow's personal
Highlight. **Schedule personal tasks for the evening or the next personal day** — set
scheduled dates so they appear in the daily planning view at morning planning. This is the
right moment for next-day decisions — energy and context are good.

**Part 2 — context break:** Do something that makes re-entering work require active effort
rather than passive drift. WFH removes the forced transition a commute provides — this
recreates it deliberately. The specific form is flexible (leave the house, eat dinner
without a screen, walk); what matters is the work context is broken. Don't rely on
willpower — change the situation so opening the laptop costs something.

---

## Evening capture sweep (~9pm) — "whole day captured, brain is free"

Capture only — review journal, brain dump anything loose to inbox, confirm tomorrow's
Highlight if not already set. No planning, no next-day decisions, no productive work
after this point.

The sweep earns the completion signal: **go to bed with a book.** Not a reward contingent
on productivity — the ritual unlocks it. Reading in bed is the end of the day, guilt-free
by design.

---

## Decision timing hierarchy

- **5pm work signoff** = good energy, right time for next-day decisions
- **9pm sweep** = capture only, no planning
- **8am** = confirmation, not deliberation

**Two shutdowns, different jobs:** Work signoff closes the professional context. The 9pm
sweep closes the cognitive loop on the whole day. Sequential, not redundant.

**Two success metrics:** Work time = output and business value. Personal time = restoration,
connection, care. Applying work metrics to personal time generates unavoidable shame.
The 9pm sweep marks the personal day complete on its own terms.
